Chart 11: Service Access Without Same-Day Registration

Are patients being served without a matching Registration? For same-day services (Pharmacy/Lab/Radiology/MCH/Specialist/Dental/Ambulance/Discharge), this checks for a Registration bill the same day. For Inpatient and Maternity (grouped by admission/IPNUMBER), it checks for any Registration bill for that patient anywhere in this 12-month extract — a narrower same-admission-window test was tried and rejected, since most admissions' registration bill falls a few days before the stay, or predates this extract entirely for returning patients (many OPNUMBERs carry prior-year prefixes). So for these two departments, "without registration" more likely reflects a patient whose original sign-up sits outside this extract's window than a live process gap — worth a sense-check against your EMR before acting on it.

Chart 12a: Length of Stay — Inpatient (IPD)

Whole period, not affected by month filter. A stay is grouped by IPNUMBER; a gap of more than 14 days between ward bills for the same IPNUMBER is treated as a new stay (readmission or a data gap), not one continuous admission — without this, a handful of extract gaps inflated some "stays" to 100+ days.

Chart 12b: Length of Stay — Maternity Ward

Whole period, not affected by month filter. Same gap-aware stay logic as Inpatient (14-day rule).

Chart 13: Average Daily Census & Bed Occupancy — Inpatient & Maternity

Average number of beds occupied per day, by month (respects the month filter above). Prefilled with your bed counts — Inpatient (IPD) = 27 (Female 6 + Male 7 + Paediatric 13 + Private 1) and Maternity = 21. Note: this extract has no ward-type field, so Inpatient bills can't be split by Female/Male/Paediatric/Private — the 27-bed occupancy figure is one blended number across all four physical wards, which can mask a full paediatric ward sitting alongside an empty male ward. Edit either box if your bed counts change.
